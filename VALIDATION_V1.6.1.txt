Important scope notes
- Production + Challenger freeze insertion is atomic in one transaction. Same-run strategy and budget artifacts carry the same prediction_run_id, but they are separate persisted artifacts rather than one cross-table database transaction.
- Missing legacy strategy shadows are deliberately not regenerated from a different candidate universe.
- The freeze hash ledger is local tamper-evidence. Protection against an adversary who can rewrite both the database and its local hashes requires an external trusted anchor.
- Self-null avg-hit and union-coverage expectations are analytic. Structure-dependent best-hit/WCE expectations use deterministic Monte Carlo (8,192 samples by default), not exhaustive mathematical enumeration.
- Fair lottery probabilities are not increased by this software; the release focuses on reducing biased evaluation and false evidence.
