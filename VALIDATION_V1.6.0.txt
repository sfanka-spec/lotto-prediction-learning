Evidence integrity
------------------
- Historical observation weight: 0.45
- Forward frozen observation weight: 1.0
- Null-anchored shared prior prevents sparse positive streaks from dominating.
- Variance floor prevents all-tie small samples from creating false certainty.
- Forward screening requires at least 30 forward draws, P(edge>0) >= 97.5%,
  a positive 95% credible-interval lower bound, and no source conflict.
- Forward confirmation requires at least 100 forward draws and P(edge>0) >= 99.5%.
- Historical-only evidence cannot reach screening or confirmation.
- Production impact: 0.0%
- Automatic promotion: disabled
- Automatic spending authority: none
