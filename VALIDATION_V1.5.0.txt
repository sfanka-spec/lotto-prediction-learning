4. Frozen Top-20 subset search
------------------------------
- Portfolio AI selects only from frozen Production Top-20.
- No number regeneration/recombination occurs in the Budget layer.
- 20 frozen lines -> 2^20 - 1 = 1,048,575 non-empty candidate subsets.
- Best subset is frozen for each line count 1..20 before the draw.
- Custom dollar budgets later map to this pre-frozen line-count frontier.

12. Known limitations / deliberate non-claims
----------------------------------------------
- A fair lottery draw remains random; high model score is not higher official draw probability.
- Current Crowd Pressure is not measured sales volume.
- Exact monetary ROI for variable prize tiers requires official prize-breakdown ingestion;
  until then the software reports a lower-bound ROI rather than fabricating a payout.
- Budget learning needs real future pre-draw freezes; historical post-draw reconstruction is
  not promoted into forward evidence.
- No Martingale/loss chasing is implemented.
