7. ROI correctness
------------------
Main-number payout and true retail-package payout are now separate concepts.

LOTTO MAX:
- one model-directed line requires one $6 retail purchase
- the same purchase also contains three terminal Quick Picks
- companion Quick Pick prizes are not silently assigned $0 and are not credited to the model

LOTTO 6/49:
- the $3 Classic line also has a system-assigned Gold Ball selection
- Gold Ball value is not silently assigned $0 and is not credited to Classic-number skill

Exact package ROI is produced only when package_payout_by_rank supplies the total
terminal/receipt payout for every selected purchase. Otherwise package ROI is NOT_AVAILABLE.
The Budget learner therefore cannot call Main-line ROI an exact investment ROI.

11. Remaining research limitations
----------------------------------
No code change can establish a lottery-prediction edge from historical patterns alone.
All number features remain heuristics until forward-frozen comparisons beat Random controls
with adequate samples and multiple-testing correction.

The empirical pair feature is intentionally NOT changed in this hardening release. It measures
historical association after marginal-frequency normalization; it is not an official fair-draw
probability multiplier. Any alternative pair null model must first run as a Shadow/A-B model.

Jackpot crowd pressure remains a jackpot-state proxy until verified sales or issued-selection
counts are available.
